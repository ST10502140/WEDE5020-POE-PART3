Our Story

SM Poultry is a small-scale poultry farming business founded in 2025 by Siyasanga Mananga with a vision to provide high-quality, fresh, and affordable poultry products to our local community.


What started as a small local venture has grown into a structured farming operation managing the full production cycle. We nurture day-old chicks, produce point-of-lay hens, and deliver table-ready Cornish and Broiler chickens.

Our Mission & Values

Quality & Health

We maintain strict biosecurity and hygiene standards to ensure healthy stock and clean farm-fresh produce.

Affordability

We keep our pricing competitive so smallholders, local households, and food vendors get high-value stock without overpaying.

Reliability

We aim to build long-term relationships with our clients through dependable supply and honest service.
