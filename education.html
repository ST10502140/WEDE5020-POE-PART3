Poultry Farming Resources

Practical advice and tips for small-scale farmers and homesteaders

1. Brooding Day-Old Chicks: Key Success Factors

The first 7–14 days are critical for chick survival. Keep these essentials in mind:

  • Temperature: Maintain brooding heat (around 32°C–35°C in the first week) using heat lamps or gas brooders.
  • Clean Water: Provide fresh water mixed with vitamins/electrolytes upon arrival.
  • Bedding: Use dry, clean wood shavings to keep chicks warm and absorb moisture.

2. Feeding Management for Fast Growth

Matching feed to growth stages ensures healthy development and optimal feed conversion:

  • Broiler Starter: High protein feed for weeks 1 to 3.
  • Broiler Grower/Finisher: Formulated for weight gain from week 4 onwards.
  • Layer Feed: Rich in calcium to support strong shell development once hens reach laying age.

3. Simple Biosecurity Rules for Small Farms

Prevent disease outbreaks before they start with basic sanitation practices:

  • Restrict visitor access to your coop area.
  • Clean and sanitize feeders and drinkers regularly.
  • Isolate sick birds immediately to prevent disease spread.
